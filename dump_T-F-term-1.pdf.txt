Below is Part 1: Physical & Chemical Changes -True or False questions.  
 
topic 1 – TRUE OR FALSE 
Physical & Chemical Changes 
1. Rotting tomatoes is a chemical change because new substances are formed. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Rotting involves decomposition, producing new substances. 
 
2. Crushing chunks of ice is a chemical change because its shape changes. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Crushing ice only changes its size and shape. The substance remains water. 
 
3. A chemical change produces one or more new substances. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: The chemical composition changes during a chemical change. 
 
4. Photosynthesis is a physical change because plants only absorb sunlight. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Photosynthesis is a chemical reaction that produces glucose and oxygen. 
 
5. Gas bubbles produced when vinegar reacts with baking soda are evidence of a chemical reaction. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: The bubbles are carbon dioxide gas formed during the reaction. 
 
6. Curdling of milk is considered evidence of gas formation. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: It is evidence of precipitate formation, where solid curds are produced. 
 
7. If a container becomes hot after quicklime is mixed with water, a chemical reaction has occurred. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: The release of heat indicates an exothermic chemical reaction. 
 
8. Simply cutting paper is evidence that a chemical reaction occurred. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Cutting only changes the paper's shape, not its composition. 
 
9. When two clear liquids produce a solid that settles at the bottom, a precipitate has formed. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: A precipitate is an insoluble solid formed during a reaction. 
 
10. Reactants are written on the right side of a chemical equation. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Reactants are written on the left side of the reaction arrow. 
 
11. Products are the new substances formed during a chemical reaction. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Products appear on the right side of the chemical equation. 
 
12. The symbol (aq) means the substance is in the gaseous state. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: (aq) means the substance is dissolved in water. 
 
13. The symbol Δ above a reaction arrow means heat is supplied to the reaction. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Delta (Δ) indicates that heating is required. 
 
14. A downward arrow (↓) beside a product indicates that a gas is released. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: A downward arrow indicates the formation of a precipitate. 
 
15. The plus (+) sign in a chemical equation means the substances are mixed or react together. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: It is read as "reacts with" or "and." 
 
16. A combination reaction follows the general equation A + B → AB. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Two or more reactants combine to form a single product. 
 
17. The reaction 2Na + Cl₂ → 2NaCl is an example of decomposition. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: It is a combination (synthesis) reaction because two reactants form one product. 
 
18. The reaction CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ is a decomposition reaction. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: One compound breaks down into two simpler substances. 
 
19. The general equation AB → A + B represents a combination reaction. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: It represents decomposition. 
 
20. In a single displacement reaction, one element replaces another element in a compound. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: This is the defining characteristic of single displacement. 
 
21. Double displacement reactions involve one element replacing another element. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: They involve two compounds exchanging ions. 
 
22. The reaction AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃ is a double displacement reaction. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: The ions exchange partners to form new compounds. 
 
23. Oxygen gas is always a reactant in combustion reactions. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Combustion requires oxygen to occur. 
 
24. Combustion of hydrocarbons always produces salt and water. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: It produces carbon dioxide and water. 
 
25. Neutralization reactions produce salt and water. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: They occur when an acid reacts with a base. 
 
26. Neutralization is classified as a special type of combination reaction. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: It is a special type of double displacement reaction. 
 
27. Melting wax is an example of a physical change. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Only the state changes; no new substance is formed. 
 
28. Salt dissolving in water is evidence of a chemical reaction. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Dissolving salt is a physical change. 
 
29. In the equation Mg + O₂ → MgO, magnesium oxide is the product. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Products are found on the right side of the arrow. 
 
30. Fuel reacting with oxygen to produce carbon dioxide and water is called combustion. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: This is the general form of a combustion reaction. 
 
31. The arrow (→) in a chemical equation means "yields" or "produces." 
Answer: True 
Explanation: It separates reactants from products. 
 
32. The reaction 2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂ is a synthesis reaction. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: It is a decomposition reaction because one compound breaks apart. 
 
33. Double displacement reactions involve the exchange of ions between two compounds. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: The ions switch partners to form new compounds. 
 
34. Iron reacting with sulfur to form iron sulfide is an example of synthesis. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Two reactants combine to form one compound. 
 
35. The symbol (g) or an upward arrow (↑) indicates that a solid has formed. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: It indicates that a gas is produced. 
 
TRUE OR FALSE : topic 3 :Acids, Bases, and Salts 
1. All acids have a sour taste and turn blue litmus paper red. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: These are characteristic properties of acids. 
 
2. Bases turn blue litmus paper red. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Bases turn red litmus paper blue. 
 
3. Pure water has a pH of 7, making it neutral. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: A pH of 7 indicates a neutral solution. 
 
4. Phenolphthalein is pink in acidic solutions. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Phenolphthalein is colorless in acids and pink in bases. 
 
5. Acids release hydrogen ions (H⁺) when dissolved in water. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: This follows the Arrhenius definition of acids. 
 
6. Bases release hydrogen ions (H⁺) in water. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Bases release hydroxide ions (OH⁻). 
 
7. Sodium chloride (NaCl) is the chemical name for common table salt. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Sodium chloride is a neutral salt commonly used as table salt. 
 
8. Hydrochloric acid found in the stomach has the formula H₂SO₄. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: The correct formula is HCl. 
 
9. Sodium hydroxide (NaOH) is a strong base commonly known as lye. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: NaOH is also called caustic soda or lye. 
 
10. Methyl orange is yellow in acids and red in bases. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Methyl orange is red in acids and yellow in bases. 
 
11. Turmeric is considered a natural acid-base indicator. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Turmeric changes color in basic solutions. 
 
12. A solution with a pH of 7 is acidic. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: A pH of 7 is neutral. 
 
13. Vinegar contains acetic acid. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Acetic acid gives vinegar its sour taste. 
 
14. Milk of magnesia contains sodium hydroxide. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: It contains magnesium hydroxide, Mg(OH)₂. 
 
15. Blue litmus paper remains blue when placed in a basic solution. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Bases do not change the color of blue litmus paper. 
 
16. Hydrochloric acid, vinegar, and calamansi juice are all examples of acids. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: All three exhibit acidic properties. 
 
17. Sodium hydroxide, bleach, and milk of magnesia are examples of bases. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: These substances are classified as bases. 
 
18. Antacids relieve hyperacidity because they are weak acids. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Antacids are mild bases that neutralize excess stomach acid. 
 
19. A solution with a pH of 4 is acidic. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Any pH below 7 is acidic. 
 
20. A solution with a pH of 11 is acidic. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: A pH above 7 indicates a basic or alkaline solution. 
 
21. Among pH 1, pH 3, and pH 6, pH 6 is the weakest acid. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: It is closest to neutral (pH 7). 
 
22. A neutralization reaction produces salt and water from an acid and a base. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: This is the general equation for neutralization. 
 
23. Turmeric turns reddish-brown when exposed to a base such as soap. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: This color change indicates the presence of a base. 
 
24. Onion and vanilla are examples of synthetic indicators. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: They are olfactory indicators because they change smell in acids and bases. 
 
25. Tooth enamel begins to dissolve when the pH in the mouth drops below 5.5. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Low pH increases the risk of tooth decay. 
 
26. Sodium carbonate (Na₂CO₃) forms an acidic solution in water. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: It forms a basic solution. 
 
27. Ammonium chloride (NH₄Cl) forms an acidic solution when dissolved in water . 
Answer: True 
Explanation: NH₄Cl is an acidic salt. 
 
28. Dry hydrogen chloride gas changes dry blue litmus paper to red immediately. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Water is needed for HCl to release hydrogen ions. 
 
29. Each pH unit represents a tenfold change in hydrogen ion concentration. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: The pH scale is logarithmic. 
 
30. In the order pH 2, pH 6, pH 7, pH 9, and pH 12, pH 12 is the strongest acid. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: pH 12 is the strongest base, while pH 2 is the strongest acid. 
 
31. Sodium chloride (NaCl) is produced from a strong acid and a strong base and is generally neutral. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: HCl reacts with NaOH to produce NaCl and water. 
 
32. Acidic and basic solutions can conduct electricity because they contain mobile ions. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: The movement of ions allows electric current to flow. 
 
33. When diluting concentrated acid, water should always be poured into the acid. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Acid should always be added slowly to water to prevent splashing. 
 
34. Acetic acid (CH₃COOH) is the weak acid present in vinegar. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: It is responsible for vinegar's acidic properties. 
 
35. Baking soda reacts with acids to release oxygen gas. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: It releases carbon dioxide (CO₂) gas during the reaction. 
 
 
 
 
Topic  4 – TRUE OR FALSE : Chemical Equations 
1. Reactants are written on the left side of a chemical equation. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Reactants are the starting substances before the reaction occurs. 
 
2. Products are found on the left side of the reaction arrow. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Products are written on the right side of the arrow because they are formed after the reaction. 
 
3. The symbol (aq) means a substance is dissolved in water. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: "Aqueous" means the substance is in a water solution. 
 
4. The symbol (s) represents a gas. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: (s) stands for solid. 
 
5. The symbol (g) indicates that a substance is in the gaseous state. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: (g) is the standard symbol for gases. 
 
6. The Law of Conservation of Mass states that mass can be created during a chemical reaction. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Mass is neither created nor destroyed; the total mass of reactants equals the total mass of products. 
 
7. The arrow (→) in a chemical equation is read as "yields" or "produces." 
Answer: True 
Explanation: It separates the reactants from the products. 
 
8. The plus (+) sign between reactants means "reacts with" or "and." 
Answer: True 
Explanation: It indicates that substances are combined in the reaction. 
 
9. A balanced chemical equation has the same number of atoms of each element on both sides. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: This follows the Law of Conservation of Mass. 
 
10. The balanced equation for forming water is H₂ + O → H₂O. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: The correct balanced equation is 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O. 
 
11. The symbol (l) indicates that a substance is in the liquid state. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: (l) stands for liquid. 
 
12. A coefficient tells the number of molecules or moles involved in a reaction. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: It multiplies the entire chemical formula. 
 
13. A subscript tells the number of molecules present in a reaction. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: A subscript tells the number of atoms of a particular element in one molecule or formula unit. 
 
14. Carbon dioxide has the chemical formula CO₂. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: It contains one carbon atom and two oxygen atoms. 
 
15. In CaCl₂, the subscript "2" refers to the number of chlorine atoms. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: There are two chlorine atoms bonded to one calcium atom. 
 
16. When balancing chemical equations, only coefficients should be changed. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Changing subscripts changes the identity of the substance. 
 
17. In 3H₂O, the number 3 is a subscript. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: The number 3 is a coefficient, while the 2 is a subscript. 
 
18. The equation Ca + Cl₂ → CaCl₂ is already balanced. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: There is one calcium atom and two chlorine atoms on both sides. 
 
19. In 2H₂SO₄, there are four hydrogen atoms in total. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Two molecules each contain two hydrogen atoms (2 × 2 = 4). 
 
20. In 4Al₂O₃, there are eight oxygen atoms altogether . 
Answer: False 
Explanation: There are 12 oxygen atoms (4 × 3 = 12). 
 
21. In the equation Mg + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂, the product side contains two chlorine atoms. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: The subscript 2 in MgCl₂ represents two chlorine atoms. 
 
22. The first step in balancing a chemical equation is to count the atoms on both sides. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Counting atoms helps determine which coefficients need adjustment. 
 
23. Chemical equations are balanced mainly to make them easier to read. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: They are balanced to obey the Law of Conservation of Mass. 
 
24. If gas escapes from an open container during a reaction, the Law of Conservation of Mass is still valid. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: The gas escaped the container, but the total mass of all matter remains unchanged. 
 
25. In the equation N₂ + H₂ → NH₃, both nitrogen and hydrogen atoms are initially unbalanced. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Both elements must be balanced by adjusting coefficients. 
 
26. In 5C₆H₁₂O₆, there are 30 carbon atoms in total. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Five molecules each contain six carbon atoms (5 × 6 = 30). 
 
27. Changing subscripts is an acceptable method for balancing chemical equations. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Changing subscripts changes the chemical substance itself. 
 
28. The balanced equation for forming water is 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: This equation has equal numbers of hydrogen and oxygen atoms on both sides. 
 
29. The balanced equation N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ correctly follows the Law of Conservation of Mass. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Both sides contain two nitrogen atoms and six hydrogen atoms. 
 
30. The balanced equation 2Al + O₂ → Al₂O₃ correctly balances aluminum and oxygen atoms. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: The correct balanced equation is 4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃. 
 
31. The balanced equation 3Fe + 4H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + 4H₂ obeys the Law of Conservation of Mass. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: The number of each type of atom is equal on both sides. 
 
32. In the balanced equation 2C₂H₆ + 7O₂ → 4CO₂ + 6H₂O, there are 14 oxygen atoms on the reactant side. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Seven oxygen molecules contain 14 oxygen atoms. 
 
33. If 12 g of carbon reacts completely with 32 g of oxygen, the total mass of carbon dioxide produced is 44 g. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: The masses of the reactants add up to the mass of the products. 
 
34. Balanced chemical equations show that atoms are rearranged, not created or destroyed. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: This is the principle behind the Law of Conservation of Mass. 
 
35. Changing subscripts while balancing an equation is acceptable because it does not change the identity of a substance. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Changing subscripts changes the chemical formula and creates a different substance. 
 
 
 
 
TOPIC 5 – TRUE OR FALSE 
Chemical Equations 
1. Reactants are the substances present before a chemical reaction begins. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Reactants are the starting materials found on the left side of the equation. 
 
2. Products are written on the left side of the reaction arrow. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Products are written on the right side because they are formed after the reaction. 
 
3. The reaction arrow (→) means "reacts to form" or "produces." 
Answer: True 
Explanation: It shows the direction of the chemical reaction. 
 
4. The symbol (s) indicates that a substance is dissolved in water. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: (s) means the substance is in the solid state. 
 
5. The symbol (aq) means the substance is dissolved in water. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: "Aqueous" refers to a solution in water. 
 
6. A coefficient is the small number written below an element symbol. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: A coefficient is the large number placed before a chemical formula. 
 
7. A subscript tells the number of atoms of an element in one molecule or formula unit. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Subscripts are part of a compound's chemical formula. 
 
8. The Law of Conservation of Mass states that the total mass of the reactants equals the total mass of the products. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Atoms are rearranged but are neither created nor destroyed. 
 
9. A combination reaction produces one product from two or more reactants. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Its general form is A + B → AB. 
 
10. A decomposition reaction combines two substances to form one product. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Decomposition involves one compound breaking into simpler substances. 
 
11. A catalyst speeds up a reaction without being used up or permanently changed. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Catalysts lower the activation energy of a reaction. 
 
12. An exothermic reaction absorbs heat from its surroundings. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Exothermic reactions release heat or light energy. 
 
13. Endothermic reactions absorb energy from their surroundings. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: These reactions require an input of energy. 
 
14. In a single displacement reaction, two compounds exchange ions. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Single displacement involves one element replacing another in a compound. 
 
15. Double displacement reactions involve two compounds exchanging ions. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: The products are formed by swapping ions between compounds. 
 
16. Oxygen gas is always a reactant in combustion reactions. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Combustion requires oxygen to occur. 
 
17. Chemical equations are used only to represent physical changes. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: They represent chemical changes where new substances are formed. 
 
18. The symbol Δ above a reaction arrow means that heat is applied. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Delta (Δ) indicates heating during the reaction. 
 
19. Rusting of iron is a physical change because no new substance is produced. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Rusting is a chemical change that forms iron oxide. 
 
20. Word equations use names of substances, while chemical equations use formulas and symbols. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Chemical equations provide a more precise representation of reactions. 
 
21. An insoluble solid formed during a reaction is called a precipitate. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Precipitates are often indicated by (s) or a downward arrow (↓). 
 
22. Subscripts can be changed when balancing equations because they do not affect the identity of a substance. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Changing a subscript changes the chemical formula and creates a different substance. 
 
23. A coefficient applies to the entire chemical formula that follows it. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: It multiplies every atom in the formula. 
 
24. A typical chemical equation directly shows how fast a reaction occurs. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: It shows the substances involved, not the reaction rate. 
 
25. A reversible reaction is represented by the symbol ⇌. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: This symbol indicates the reaction can proceed in both directions. 
 
26. Even if a chemical equation is not balanced, it can still identify the reactants, products, and reaction type. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Balancing affects quantity, not the identity of the substances. 
 
27. The reaction arrow (→) means the reactants and products are always equal in every way. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: The arrow indicates the direction of the reaction, not equality. 
 
28. The symbol (g) beside a product indicates that a gas is produced. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: (g) represents the gaseous state. 
 
29. Chemical formulas are used because they show the types and numbers of atoms in a substance. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Formulas provide exact information about chemical composition. 
 
30. The main purpose of a chemical equation is to summarize a chemical reaction using standard symbols and formulas. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: It clearly shows the reactants, products, and direction of the reaction.

Topic 6 – TRUE OR FALSE 
Rates of Chemical Reactions 
1. Reaction rate refers to how fast a chemical reaction takes place. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: The rate of reaction measures how quickly reactants are converted into products. 
 
2. Increasing the temperature usually slows down the rate of a chemical reaction. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Higher temperatures increase particle movement, leading to more frequent and energetic collisions. 
 
3. A catalyst speeds up a chemical reaction without being permanently consumed. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Catalysts lower the activation energy needed for a reaction. 
 
4. Endothermic reactions release heat to their surroundings. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Endothermic reactions absorb heat from their surroundings. 
 
5. Exothermic reactions release heat energy into the surroundings. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Combustion and neutralization are common examples of exothermic reactions. 
 
6. Increasing the surface area of a solid reactant generally increases the reaction rate. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: More surface area allows more particles to collide at the same time. 
 
7. The fire triangle is made up of fuel, oxygen, and carbon dioxide. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: The fire triangle consists of fuel, oxygen, and heat. 
 
8. Refrigeration preserves food by slowing down chemical reactions and microbial activity. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Lower temperatures reduce the rate of spoilage reactions. 
 
9. An inhibitor increases the rate of a chemical reaction. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Inhibitors slow down or prevent chemical reactions. 
 
10. Burning wood is an example of an exothermic reaction. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Burning releases heat and light energy. 
 
11. Activation energy is the minimum amount of energy required for a reaction to occur. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Reacting particles must overcome this energy barrier. 
 
12. According to collision theory, every collision between particles produces a reaction. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Only effective collisions with enough energy and proper orientation produce reactions. 
 
13. Crushing a tablet into powder usually makes it dissolve faster. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Crushing increases surface area, allowing faster interaction with the solvent. 
 
14. Larger pieces of charcoal burn faster than powdered charcoal. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Powdered charcoal has greater surface area and burns more quickly. 
 
15. Lowering the temperature decreases the reaction rate of most chemical reactions. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Lower temperatures reduce particle movement and collision frequency. 
 
16. Powdered calcium carbonate reacts more quickly with hydrochloric acid than marble chips. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Powder has a much greater surface area exposed to the acid. 
 
17. Increasing the concentration of reactants generally increases the rate of reaction. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: More particles are available to collide each second. 
 
18. Enzymes are biological catalysts found in living organisms. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: They speed up biochemical reactions without being consumed. 
 
19. Water extinguishes most fires mainly by removing oxygen from the flame. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Water primarily cools the fuel below its ignition temperature. 
 
20. A fire blanket extinguishes fire by cutting off the oxygen supply. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Smothering the fire prevents oxygen from reaching it. 
 
21. Painting iron helps prevent rusting by reducing contact with oxygen and water. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: The paint acts as a protective barrier. 
 
22. Salting and sugaring preserve food by reducing the amount of available water for microorganisms. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Microorganisms need water to survive and multiply. 
 
23. Canning preserves food mainly by increasing its surface area. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Canning uses heat to destroy microorganisms and seals the food to prevent contamination. 
 
24. Stirring reactants can increase the rate of a chemical reaction. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Stirring brings reactant particles into contact more frequently. 
 
25. Photosynthesis is an endothermic reaction because it absorbs energy from sunlight. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Light energy is required to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose. 
 
26. Higher temperatures increase reaction rates only because particles collide more often. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: They also increase the number of particles with enough energy to overcome activation energy. 
 
27. Catalysts provide an alternative reaction pathway with lower activation energy. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: This allows reactions to occur more quickly. 
 
28. Photosynthesis is an exothermic reaction because it releases heat. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Photosynthesis absorbs energy, making it endothermic. 
 
29. Explosions are examples of extremely fast chemical reactions. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: They occur rapidly and release large amounts of energy in a short time. 
 
30. A chemical reaction stops when all of the reactants have been consumed. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Without reactants, no further reaction can occur. 
 
31. Catalytic converters reduce harmful vehicle emissions by using catalysts. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: They convert harmful gases into less harmful substances before they are released. 
 
32. Increasing the amount of oxygen can make combustion occur more rapidly. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: More oxygen increases the frequency of effective combustion reactions. 
 
33. Galvanizing iron protects it from corrosion by coating it with zinc. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Zinc acts as a protective layer and corrodes before the iron does. 
 
Topic  7 – TRUE OR FALSE 
Homeostasis 
1. Homeostasis is the maintenance of a stable internal environment despite changes in the external environment. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Homeostasis maintains dynamic internal balance even when external conditions change. 
 
2. Homeostasis means the body remains completely unchanged at all times. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: The body constantly makes small adjustments to keep conditions within a normal range. 
 
3. The nervous and endocrine systems are the main control systems involved in homeostasis. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: The nervous system responds quickly, while the endocrine system provides longer-lasting regulation. 
 
4. Sweating when body temperature rises is an example of positive feedback. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Sweating is a negative feedback mechanism because it helps return body temperature to normal. 
 
5. The normal target value maintained by homeostasis is called the set point. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Examples include normal body temperature and blood glucose level. 
 
6. Effectors detect changes in the body's internal environment. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Receptors detect changes, while effectors carry out the response. 
 
7. Muscles and glands are examples of effectors in homeostasis. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: They perform actions that help restore normal conditions. 
 
8. Negative feedback increases a change farther away from the set point. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Negative feedback reverses the change and restores balance. 
 
9. Blood glucose, blood pH, and body temperature are regulated by homeostasis. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: These are important internal conditions that must remain within healthy limits. 
 
10. Human height is maintained at a constant value through homeostasis. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Height naturally changes during growth and is not regulated as a homeostatic variable. 
 
11. The hypothalamus is the primary control center for body temperature regulation. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: It receives information from receptors and coordinates appropriate responses. 
 
12. When you are cold, the blood vessels in your skin widen to release more heat. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: They constrict to reduce heat loss. 
 
13. Hormones are chemical messengers produced by the endocrine system. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Hormones help regulate many body processes over longer periods. 
 
14. Insulin and glucagon work together to regulate blood glucose levels. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: They have opposite effects that keep blood sugar within the normal range. 
 
15. When blood sugar rises, the pancreas releases glucagon. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: The pancreas releases insulin to lower blood glucose levels. 
 
16. When blood sugar falls, the pancreas releases glucagon. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Glucagon stimulates the release of stored glucose from the liver. 
 
17. The kidneys help maintain the body's water and salt balance. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: They adjust urine concentration to regulate fluid balance. 
 
18. Shivering is an example of negative feedback that helps restore body temperature. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Muscle contractions generate heat to raise body temperature. 
 
19. Maintaining a constant body weight is one of the main goals of homeostasis. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Body weight naturally changes and is not a fixed homeostatic set point. 
 
20. Insulin lowers blood glucose, while glucagon raises it. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: These hormones have opposite actions to maintain stable blood sugar. 
 
21. Positive feedback amplifies a change instead of reversing it. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Positive feedback continues until a specific event is completed. 
 
22. Blood clotting is an example of positive feedback. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Clotting chemicals activate more clotting until the wound is sealed. 
 
23. Homeostasis requires energy to maintain stable internal conditions. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Active processes are needed to continuously regulate the body's internal environment. 
 
24. If blood pH is not properly regulated, enzymes may stop functioning correctly. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Incorrect pH can change enzyme shape and reduce their activity. 
 
25. During exercise, breathing rate increases to help maintain oxygen and carbon dioxide balance. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Faster breathing supplies more oxygen and removes excess carbon dioxide. 
 
26. The correct order of a homeostatic feedback loop is: Stimulus → Receptor → Control Center → Effector → Response. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: This is the standard sequence of negative and positive feedback mechanisms. 
 
27. Oxytocin causing stronger contractions during childbirth is an example of negative feedback. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: It is positive feedback because the contractions become stronger until birth occurs. 
 
28. Long-term failure of homeostasis can lead to disease, organ damage, or death. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Cells cannot function properly when internal conditions move outside safe limits. 
 
29. Dehydration causes the kidneys to reabsorb more water into the bloodstream. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: This helps restore normal water balance through negative feedback. 
 
30. When blood pressure rises above normal, the body attempts to lower it back to its set point. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Negative feedback mechanisms work to return blood pressure to normal. 
 
31. Dynamic equilibrium means the body's internal conditions never change. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Dynamic equilibrium allows small fluctuations while remaining within a healthy range. 
 
32. Diabetes is primarily associated with a failure in blood glucose regulation. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: It results from problems with insulin production or insulin action. 
 
33. Without homeostasis, cells and organs cannot function properly. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Failure to maintain stable internal conditions can lead to severe damage or death. 
 
Topic 8 – TRUE OR FALSE 
Evolution 
1. Evolution is the gradual change in the inherited traits of a population over many generations. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Evolution occurs in populations over time through the inheritance of genetic traits. 
 
2. Evolution occurs when an individual organism changes its traits during its lifetime. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Individuals may grow or adapt, but evolution occurs across generations in populations. 
 
3. Charles Darwin proposed natural selection as the primary mechanism of evolution. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Darwin introduced the theory of natural selection in On the Origin of Species (1859). 
 
4. Gregor Mendel developed the theory of natural selection. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Mendel is known for his work on heredity and genetics, not natural selection. 
 
5. Natural selection favors organisms that are better adapted to their environment. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Better-adapted organisms are more likely to survive and reproduce. 
 
6. The strongest organism always survives through natural selection. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Fitness refers to successful reproduction, not simply physical strength. 
 
7. An adaptation is an inherited trait that increases an organism's chance of survival and reproduction. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Adaptations improve fitness in a particular environment. 
 
8. Variation refers to similarities among individuals of the same species. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Variation describes the differences in inherited traits among individuals. 
 
9. Heredity allows useful traits to be passed from parents to offspring. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Evolution depends on inherited genetic traits. 
 
10. Fossils are preserved remains or traces of ancient organisms. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Fossils provide evidence of organisms that lived long ago. 
 
11. Homologous structures have different evolutionary origins but identical functions. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Homologous structures share a common ancestry but may have different functions. 
 
12. The human arm, whale flipper, and bat wing are examples of homologous structures. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: They share the same underlying bone structure inherited from a common ancestor. 
 
13. Vestigial structures are body parts that have lost most or all of their original function. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Examples include the human appendix and tailbone. 
 
14. Biogeography is the study of fossils found in sedimentary rocks. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Biogeography studies the distribution of organisms across Earth. 
 
15. The peppered moth is a classic example of natural selection in action. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Dark-colored moths became more common during the Industrial Revolution because they were better 
camouflaged. 
 
16. Artificial selection occurs when nature determines which traits are inherited. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Artificial selection is controlled by humans through selective breeding. 
 
17. Geographic isolation occurs when a physical barrier separates populations. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Mountains, rivers, or oceans can prevent populations from interbreeding. 
 
18. Bird wings and insect wings are homologous structures. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: They are analogous structures because they have similar functions but different evolutionary origins. 
 
19. Analogous structures perform similar functions but evolved independently. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: They do not indicate a recent common ancestor. 
 
20. Isolation can contribute to the formation of new species. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Preventing gene flow allows populations to evolve independently. 
 
21. Fossils found in deeper rock layers are generally older than fossils found in upper layers. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Lower rock layers were formed earlier in Earth's history. 
 
22. Giraffes developed long necks because they stretched them during their lifetime. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Long-necked individuals survived and reproduced more successfully over many generations. 
 
23. Choosing only the largest mangoes to plant is an example of artificial selection. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Humans intentionally select desirable traits. 
 
24. Similar early embryos of vertebrates provide evidence of common ancestry. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Comparative embryology supports evolutionary relationships. 
 
25. Reproductive isolation occurs only when a mountain or river separates populations. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Reproductive isolation can also result from differences in behavior, breeding season, or mating patterns. 
 
26. A species found naturally only in one geographic area is called endemic. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: The Philippine Eagle is an example of an endemic species. 
 
27. The correct sequence for speciation is Variation → Geographic Isolation → Natural Selection → Reproductive Isolation 
→ New Species. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: These processes gradually lead to the formation of new species. 
 
28. Mutation alone consistently produces adaptations that perfectly match the environment. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: Mutations create variation, but natural selection determines which traits become common. 
 
29. Vestigial pelvic bones in modern whales provide evidence that whales evolved from four-legged land mammals. 
Answer: True 
Explanation: Fossil evidence and comparative anatomy support this evolutionary history. 
 
30. In science, the word "theory" means a simple guess with little supporting evidence. 
Answer: False 
Explanation: A scientific theory is a well-tested explanation supported by extensive evidence.
